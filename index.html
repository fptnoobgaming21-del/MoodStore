Care to make it good

Three things we promise

01

Organic from the farm

Milk from organic-certified herds, no added hormones, and grass in the diet whenever the season allows. The cow on the carton is the one who made it.

02

Ultra-pasteurized, not ultra-processed

A few seconds at high heat, then straight into the carton. Unopened, it keeps in the fridge for weeks. Once opened, finish it within 7 days.

03

A carton you can recycle

Paperboard gable top with a plant-based lining. Rinse, flatten, and put it with your paper. We are working toward a fully fibre-based cap by 2027.
